# Nord (style)

From daisyUI's "nord" theme (MIT), after the Nord colour palette. Tokens:
`tokens.css`. Font: the device's system font.

**Feel:** soft snow-grey and muted arctic blue, little contrast between
surfaces, gentle status colours. Restful.
**Good for:** apps looked at often or late at night: habits, reading, calm
dashboards.

## Principles (copy into the app's DESIGN.md)
- Everything is muted; nothing glows. Status colours are soft (sage, sand, rose).
- Because surfaces are close in colour, separate them with spacing and borders.
- Dark mode is a first-class mode, not an afterthought.

## Type and shapes
- System font, headings at 600.
- 8px corners on cards, 4px on inputs and buttons; no shadows.

## Don't
- Add a bright colour "to make it pop". It breaks the calm. Use size or weight.
