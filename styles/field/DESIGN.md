# Field (style)

Inspired by PostHog's look (awesome-design-md, MIT). Not affiliated: no names,
logos or illustrations from it. Tokens: `tokens.css`. Font: IBM Plex Sans
(Google Fonts, as the source), with the system font as fallback.

**Feel:** warm greige page, white cards, olive-black text and a bright amber
button with dark text. Practical and friendly, with a bit of character.
**Good for:** hobby and outdoor apps, logs and tools used often.

## Principles (copy into the app's DESIGN.md)
- Amber is for the one main action per screen, always with dark text on it.
- Links are blue (`--link`), because amber text isn't readable on white.
- Cards have a thin bottom line instead of a soft shadow: flat and sturdy.

## Type and shapes
- IBM Plex Sans, bold headings (700), compact spacing.
- 8px corners on cards, 6px on buttons and inputs.

## Don't
- Use amber for text or small icons on white: it fails contrast.
