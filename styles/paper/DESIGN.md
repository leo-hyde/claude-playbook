# Paper (style)

Inspired by Notion's look (awesome-design-md, MIT). Not affiliated: no names,
logos or illustrations from it. Tokens: `tokens.css`. Font: Inter (Google
Fonts; Notion's own font is based on it), with the system font as fallback.

**Feel:** white page, warm off-white panels, warm-grey text and thin lines.
Quiet and document-like, with lots of air.
**Good for:** notes, lists, planners and anything mostly text.

## Principles (copy into the app's DESIGN.md)
- Structure comes from spacing and thin lines, not boxes and shadows.
- Text is warm charcoal, never pure black; secondary text is warm grey.
- Purple only for the main action; most screens have one purple thing at most.

## Type and shapes
- Inter, headings at weight 600, body at 400. Generous line height (1.5) for text.
- 12px corners on cards, 8px on inputs and buttons. Shadows barely there.

## Don't
- Fill the screen with tinted cards. One pastel panel (`--surface-2`) at a time.
