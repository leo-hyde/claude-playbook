# Night (style)

Inspired by Linear's look (awesome-design-md, MIT). Not affiliated: no names
or logos from it. Tokens: `tokens.css`. Font: Inter (Linear's own is
private), with the system font as fallback.

**Feel:** dark first: near-black page, layered charcoal panels, pale text and
one lavender-blue. Focused and quiet.
**Good for:** tools used for long stretches, and apps where dark mode is the
main mode.

## Principles (copy into the app's DESIGN.md)
- Depth comes from slightly lighter panels on black, not from shadows.
- One lavender-blue for the main action, links and the active item.
- Thin, dim borders; text is off-white, secondary text a cool grey.
- Design and check the dark theme first; the light theme follows it.

## Type and shapes
- Inter, headings at 600, compact and precise.
- 8px corners on cards, 6px on buttons and inputs.

## Don't
- Use pure white text or bright borders on black: they glare.
