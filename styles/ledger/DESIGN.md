# Ledger (style)

Inspired by Stripe's look (awesome-design-md, MIT). Not affiliated: no names,
logos or gradients from it. Tokens: `tokens.css`. Font: Inter (Stripe's own
is paid), with the system font as fallback.

**Feel:** crisp white cards on a pale blue-grey page, deep navy text and a
vivid indigo. Precise and trustworthy.
**Good for:** money, bookings, forms and anything where people need to trust
the numbers.

## Principles (copy into the app's DESIGN.md)
- Numbers are the heroes: large, navy, `tabular-nums`, with units always shown.
- Indigo for the main action and links only; everything else navy and grey.
- Forms are roomy: one column, labels above fields, clear error text under them.

## Type and shapes
- Inter, headings at 600, slightly tight letter-spacing on big numbers.
- 8px corners on cards, 6px on buttons and inputs; a crisp, cool shadow in light mode.

## Don't
- Use colour for decoration. Colour means action (indigo) or status.
