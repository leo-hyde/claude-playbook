# Clear (style)

The playbook's own style, from the Sport Conditions app. Tokens: `tokens.css`
(also the default in `templates/style.css`). Font: the device's system font.

**Feel:** cool grey page, white cards, one clear blue. Calm and neutral, so
status colours stand out.
**Good for:** utility apps that answer a question at a glance: forecasts,
trackers, dashboards.

## Principles (copy into the app's DESIGN.md)
- Cards sit on a grey page; the page itself carries no content colour.
- Blue only for the main action, links and the active tab.
- Status colours (good, OK, no) are the brightest things on screen; "no" is grey.

## Type and shapes
- System font, bold headings (700), numbers in `tabular-nums`.
- 14px corners on cards and dialogs, 6px on small chips; soft shadow in light mode only.

## Don't
- Add a second accent colour. Use the status colours or grey instead.
